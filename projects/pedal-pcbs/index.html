Guitar effects pedal PCBs

I laid out, soldered and debugged three working guitar effects pedal boards from existing schematics.

  • KiCad
GitHub

Project notes

I built three guitar effects pedal boards based on the Boss DS-1, Big Muff Pi and EHX LPB-1 between September and November 2025. I worked from existing schematics, placing components and routing the PCB layouts in KiCad, then producing Gerber files.

My LPB-1 layout fits four resistors, two capacitors and a transistor on a small board. The Big Muff Pi design has four transistor packages and labelled input, power, ground and output connections. My DS-1 design includes an eight-pin socket and a barrel power jack, visible in the KiCad 3D view.

I soldered the through-hole components and debugged the boards, applying gain staging, clipping and biasing. My work took these established circuits from PCB layout through assembly. All three boards are working.
