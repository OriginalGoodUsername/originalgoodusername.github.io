Guitar Effects Pedal PCBs

PCB design and analog electronics · Sep - Nov 2025

What it is

I turned three existing guitar effects schematics into custom PCB layouts and working hardware. The circuits were based on the Boss DS-1, Big Muff Pi and EHX LPB-1.

How it works

I used KiCad to take the existing schematics through PCB layout and Gerber output. I then soldered the through-hole components and debugged each assembled board.

The builds involved analog gain staging, clipping and biasing. My work was the PCB implementation and completed hardware, using existing effects circuit designs.

Results

All three pedals work. The repository includes the Big Muff and LPB schematics, board layouts and Gerber exports. The finished DS-1 board is missing from the available local copy; its schematic is included.

The Big Muff schematic matches the credited upstream project, whose CC0 license is retained in the repository. These builds use established circuit designs.

Tools: KiCad, Analog circuits, Soldering
