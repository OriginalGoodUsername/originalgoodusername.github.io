Project notes

I assembled an F450 quadcopter with an ESP32 flight controller running esp-fc. I wired an MPU-6500 IMU, BMP280 barometer and QMC5883L magnetometer over I2C for motion, pressure and heading measurements. I mapped a Logitech F310 USB gamepad's inputs and integrated the ESP32-S3 transmitter with the receiver to send flight and arm/disarm commands over ESP-NOW. Stabilization, sensor fusion, the PID controller and motor mixer come from esp-fc.

The F310 throttle stick springs back to centre, which means roughly half throttle in manual mode. I implemented arming with the throttle down and the right bumper pressed, disarming with the left bumper, and a limit on how quickly throttle commands change.

I calibrated all four ESCs, checked motor mapping and spin direction, and confirmed flight-channel inputs in the receiver monitor. I completed outdoor takeoff, hover and manual gamepad flight with self-level stabilization. I also built separate Arduino flight firmware that can fly this quadcopter as an alternative to esp-fc.
