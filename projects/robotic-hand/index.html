Gesture-Controlled Robotic Hand

Computer vision and actuation · Personal project

What it is

I wanted a physical robotic hand to copy hand movements from a webcam feed without requiring a glove. That meant translating visual hand landmarks into commands for six servos.

How it works

Python, OpenCV and MediaPipe process the webcam feed. I map hand-landmark geometry to finger angles before sending the commands.

The angles travel over a custom UART protocol to an Arduino. The Arduino controls a PCA9685 over I2C, with per-servo pulse calibration for the hand’s actuators.

Problems I hit

Servo jitter affected the movement. I reduced it using exponential moving-average smoothing and rate limiting in the command pipeline.

Results

The six-servo hand copies webcam hand movements in real time without a glove.

Tools: Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, Arduino, PCA9685
