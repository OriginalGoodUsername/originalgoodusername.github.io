Project notes

I designed and built a robot that combines four-leg walking with four-wheel drive. The current build uses an Arduino Nano ESP32, twelve leg servos, a PCA9685 and four DC motors driven by two TB6612 drivers. My work covered the mechanical design and integration of the walking and wheeled controls.

I adapted legacy gait code credited to SunFounder, Regis Hsu and Anuchit. Its AVR timer-based servo loop needed to change for the ESP32, so I replaced the timer-interrupt loop with a FreeRTOS task. I also integrated an ESP-NOW gamepad link. The project's hardware tools support I2C scanning, individual servo and motor tests, servo centering and calibration-pulse recording.
